Curated rarity points are estimates, not live player statistics. Daily reset: 00:00 UTC.

How to play

Seven Certamen-themed categories, one question from each. Give a valid answer that few other players might think of.

  1. Start the daily challenge or a practice run. Every game has one mythology, history, language, literature, culture, geography, and derivatives question.
  2. You have 25 seconds to enter an answer for each prompt.
  3. Unrecognized answers can be retried before time runs out.
  4. Each accepted answer receives a curated rarity score from 10 to 100. An omitted answer scores 0.
  5. Finish all seven to earn your result and share a spoiler-free score.

This is a finite educational answer bank, not an AI judge. A historically valid answer may be missing. Latin macrons, capitalization, and basic punctuation are ignored; listed alternate spellings work.
